# ZOcre Framework — Community Code of Conduct

ZOcre public spaces are intended for technical discussion, support and community interaction around ZOcre Framework.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and stay on topic;
- provide constructive technical feedback;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and personal attacks;
- avoid spam, impersonation and deliberately misleading information;
- respect software licenses, copyright and project boundaries;
- never expose private credentials, player data or non-public ZOcre material.

Moderation may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community, project security or legal rights.

## Français

Les espaces publics ZOcre sont destinés aux échanges techniques, au support et à la communauté autour de ZOcre Framework.

Les participants doivent rester respectueux, constructifs et respecter les licences, la sécurité et la frontière entre contenus publics et privés. Le harcèlement, les attaques personnelles, le spam, l'usurpation et la publication de données ou contenus privés ne sont pas acceptés.
